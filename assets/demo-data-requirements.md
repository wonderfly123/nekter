# Nekter Demo Data - 10 Account Story Arcs

## The Company Story

**DataFlow Solutions** is a mid-market data integration platform company with 150 employees and ~$25M ARR. We help enterprises connect their disparate systems - CRMs, ERPs, marketing platforms, data warehouses - through our iPaaS (integration Platform as a Service). Our customers range from 100-5000 employee companies across tech, healthcare, finance, and retail.

By late 2023, we were growing fast (40% YoY) but our Customer Success team was drowning. We had 8 CSMs managing 180 accounts each - way too many. Response times were slipping, renewals were at risk, and our NRR (Net Revenue Retention) dropped from 115% to 98% in Q3 2023. Our VP of Customer Success knew we had a problem: **our CSMs couldn't be proactive at scale.**

In December 2023, we evaluated CS platforms and chose **Nekter.io**. The promise was simple: give CSMs AI-powered insights so they could act like they're managing 20 accounts when they're actually managing 180. Nekter would surface health risks, suggest proactive actions, and help CSMs respond faster with full context.

We deployed Nekter on **May 17, 2024 (Day 150 of our fiscal year)**. This dataset tracks 10 representative accounts across the full fiscal year (Dec 2023 - Dec 2024) to show the before/after impact of Nekter on CSM performance and customer outcomes.

**Key Insight:** Customers never knew about Nekter. They just experienced dramatically better CSM support after May 2024.

## The 3 Phases

### Decline Phase (Days 1-120)
**CSM Behavior:**
- Reactive only, slow responses (24-48hrs)
- Missing context, surface-level fixes
- No proactive outreach
- Poor meeting preparation

**Customer Experience:**
- Real problems: data sync failures, performance issues, integration breaking
- High ticket volume
- Frustrated communications
- Health declining

### Implementation Phase (Days 121-180)
**CSM Behavior (Nekter impact starting):**
- Faster responses (4-8hrs)
- Better context, root cause fixes
- Some proactive outreach begins
- Improved meeting preparation

**Customer Experience:**
- Fewer tickets
- CSM catching issues early
- Cautiously optimistic
- Health stabilizing

### Growth Phase (Days 181-365)
**CSM Behavior (Nekter mastery):**
- Very fast responses (1-2hrs)
- Highly proactive, preventive
- Strategic partnership
- Data-driven, prepared meetings

**Customer Experience:**
- Low ticket volume
- Proactive CSM outreach
- Strategic planning, expansion talks
- Health improving

### accounts

CREATE TABLE public.accounts (
  id integer NOT NULL DEFAULT nextval('accounts_id_seq'::regclass),
  sf_account_id text NOT NULL UNIQUE,
  name text NOT NULL,
  type text,                          -- 'Customer' or 'Former Customer'
  arr numeric,
  industry text,
  employee_count integer,
  owner_email text,
  owner_name text,
  owner_id text,

## Health Score Calculation

```
health_score = (
  avg_sentiment_30day * 0.40 +      # 30-day weighted average sentiment
  ticket_health * 0.25 +             # Based on volume/severity/resolution time
  avg_engagement_30day * 0.20 +      # 30-day weighted average engagement
  response_adherence * 0.15          # SLA compliance/response times
)
```

**Health Status Thresholds:**
- **Healthy:** health_score >= 70
- **At Risk:** 50 <= health_score < 70
- **Critical:** health_score < 50

**Trend Calculation:**
- Compare current week to previous week
- **Improving:** +2 or more points
- **Declining:** -2 or more points
- **Stable:** Between -2 and +2 points
